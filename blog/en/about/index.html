About and editorial principles

Who writes here, what gets published and how the pieces are prepared. The rules on sources, numbers, client confidentiality and the use of AI.

Who writes here

I’m S. Kaan Özdemir. I started in 2010 building enterprise Java applications and have worked in RPA and intelligent automation since 2017. Today I’m Managing Partner and Director of Automation & AI at BOTTEQ Consulting, part of Medyasoft Bilişim Grubu. What you’ll find here

  • Weekly Radar. Five to seven items from the automation, AI and regulation news. Each links to its primary source and comes with why it matters.
  • Analysis. A clear view on one subject, with the reasoning and the sources.
  • Playbooks. A decision or a method: what to do in which situation, and when not to.
  • Use cases. Flows drawn from real projects, with step-by-step diagrams and videos.

Editorial principles

Sources. News links to its primary source: the company announcing the product, the body publishing the regulation, the analyst firm making the forecast. Secondary sources are only for explanation or corroboration. When I can’t access a source, I say so in the piece.

Numbers. No number without a source. Figures from my own projects are results that can be verified in writing.

Clients. I don’t name clients. I give enough detail to describe the sector and scale, and no more.

Opinion and fact. Facts come with sources; opinions are marked as mine. I try not to blur the two.

Use of AI. I use AI-assisted tools for research and first drafts. The Weekly Radar is compiled by an automated research pipeline in which every item links to its primary source. Analysis, playbooks and use cases are read and approved by me before they go out. The note at the end of each piece says which applies.

Corrections. If you spot a mistake, email me. Corrected pieces show an update date.

Independence. Views here are personal. They are not the official position of BOTTEQ Consulting, its partners or its clients.

Privacy

This blog sets no cookies and runs no visitor analytics. Everything, fonts included, loads from this site’s own server; opening a page sends nothing to a third party. Your theme and language choice stay in your own browser. Videos don’t load until you press play; once you do, the platform’s (YouTube or Vimeo) terms apply. As with any website, the hosting provider may keep technical access logs.
